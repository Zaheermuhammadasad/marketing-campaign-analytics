# CV and LinkedIn Project Entry

## CV version

**Marketing Campaign & Customer Analytics | SQL, Python, Pandas, Plotly**

- Analysed a public customer-marketing dataset with 2,240 customers and 29 fields, including product spend, purchase behaviour, demographics, and campaign response.
- Built data-quality checks for missing income, duplicate customer identifiers, and implausible birth-year records.
- Compared the latest campaign response with five historical acceptance fields; latest response was 14.91% in the observed dataset.
- Analysed 1,356,988 in recorded spending across six product groups, identifying wine and meat as the largest groups.
- Developed SQL queries, a reproducible Pandas workflow, an interactive dashboard, and documented limitations around causal lift and marketing ROI.

GitHub: https://github.com/Zaheermuhammadasad/marketing-campaign-analytics

## LinkedIn

Built an end-to-end marketing analytics project using SQL, Python/Pandas, and Plotly. Analysed customer spending, product preferences, purchase-channel counts, data quality, historical campaign acceptance, and latest campaign response. Created reproducible output tables, an interactive dashboard, and testable business recommendations with clear analytical caveats.

The public dataset contains 2,240 customers; the observed latest campaign response is 14.91%. This is descriptive response, not causal lift: the source lacks advertising spend, impressions, clicks, and randomized holdout data.

Project: https://github.com/Zaheermuhammadasad/marketing-campaign-analytics
