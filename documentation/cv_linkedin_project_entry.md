# CV and LinkedIn Project Entry

## CV

**Marketing Campaign & Customer Analytics | SQL, Python, Pandas, Plotly**

- Analysed a public marketing/customer dataset covering 2,240 customers and 29 fields, including purchase behaviour, campaign response, spending, and customer attributes.
- Built quality checks for missing income, duplicate IDs, and implausible birth years.
- Compared current campaign responses with five historical acceptance fields; latest-campaign response was 14.91% in this sample.
- Analysed 1,356,988 in recorded spend across six product groups, with wine and meat as the largest categories.
- Developed SQL queries, a reproducible Pandas pipeline, an interactive dashboard, and documented limitations around causal lift and ROI.

GitHub: https://github.com/Zaheermuhammadasad/marketing-campaign-analytics

## LinkedIn

Built an end-to-end marketing analytics project using SQL, Python/Pandas, and Plotly. Analysed customer spending, product preferences, purchase-channel counts, data quality, historical acceptance, and latest campaign response. Created reproducible outputs, an interactive dashboard and testable recommendations, documenting limitations.

The 2,240-customer dataset has a latest campaign response rate of 14.91% in the observed sample. This is descriptive, not causal lift; the data lacks advertising spend, impressions/clicks and a randomized holdout.

Project: https://github.com/Zaheermuhammadasad/marketing-campaign-analytics
